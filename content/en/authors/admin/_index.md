I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at <a href="https://www.mcgill.ca/about/history" target="_blank">McGill University</a> and <a href="https://mila.quebec/en/" target="_blank">Mila - Quebec AI Institute</a> (one of the world's largest AI communities founded by <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=kukA0LcAAAAJ&hl=en" target="_blank">Yoshua Bengio</a>).
I am delighted in working with <a href="https://cs.mcgill.ca/~xueliu/site/intro.html" target="_blank">Steve Liu</a> and <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=212SLn0AAAAJ" target="_blank">Fernando Diaz</a>.
I also collaborate with <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Cul0g2YAAAAJ&hl=en" target="_blank">Laurent Charlin</a> and <a href="https://scholar.google.ca/citations?user=CEt6_mMAAAAJ&hl=en" target="_blank">Joelle Pineau</a>, and have them on my supervisory committee.

My research interests focus on <b>learning from human feedback for personalized information access</b> and lie at the intersection of personalization, evaluation, and responsibility.
Generally, I am interested in and have experience with: <b>(i) user modeling and preference elicitation</b>, <b>(ii) knowledge representation and retrieval</b>, and <b>(iii) alignment to human value and social good (e.g., diversity, fairness, interpretability, transparency, etc)</b>.
Additionally, I love interdisciplinary research and am particularly interested in applying AI techniques to intelligent tutoring systems for personalized education.

During my Ph.D., I interned and collaborated closely with <b>Google Research</b> and <b>Microsoft Research</b>.
My research is generously supported by <a href="https://www.mcgill.ca/expmed/funding-and-awards/internal-awards" target="_blank">McGill Graduate Excellence Award</a>, <a href="https://www.borealisai.com/news/the-borealis-ai-2022-2023-fellowships-fostering-next-gen-research-talent/" target="_blank">Borealis AI Fellowship</a>, and <a href="https://frq.gouv.qc.ca/en/" target="_blank">FRQNT PhD Scholarship</a> (ranked 1st place).
