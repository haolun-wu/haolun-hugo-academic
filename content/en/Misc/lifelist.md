* Born and raised in <b><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chengdu">Chengdu</a></b>, a city renowned for its comfort, tantalizing cuisine, and a rich history spanning over 4,000 years, I spent my childhood surrounded by the unique cultural heritage of my hometown. My journey began in this city of pandas 🐼, Szechuan spicy cuisine 🌶, mahjong 🀄, and traditional tea houses 🍵, before I ventured across the globe.
* Music has been a significant part of my life; I am a <b><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarinet">clarinet</a></b> player. I was an active member of the Sichuan Musicians Association, under the mentorship of <a href="https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E6%9C%B1%E6%AF%85/3178725">Yi Zhu</a>, presently the Deputy Director of the Music Department at Sichuan University.
* My artistic pursuits also extend to <b><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_calligraphy">Chinese Calligraphy</a></b>, where I have achieved mastery. I had the privilege of being advised by <a href="https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E9%83%91%E5%AE%B6%E6%9E%97/8612136">Jialin Zheng</a>, who currently serves as the Vice Chairman of both the Sichuan and Chengdu Calligraphers Associations.
* I met <b><a href="https://kathyhuang.netlify.app/">my lovely wife</a></b> 💌 in San Diego, California, when we were both 20. Our journey together took us from marriage in Montreal to a romantic honeymoon in Quebec City. We have a daughter now.
